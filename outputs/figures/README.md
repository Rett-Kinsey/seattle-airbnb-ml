# Exported EDA Figures

This directory contains the **7 exploratory data analysis (EDA) figures** extracted directly (byte-for-byte) from the saved outputs of [`notebooks/03_exploratory_data_analysis.ipynb`](../../notebooks/03_exploratory_data_analysis.ipynb). These standalone PNG files (`150 DPI`) are prepared for team review and direct inclusion in the Progress Report.

| File Name | EDA Block & Section | Description & Key Metrics Shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01_price_distribution_histogram.png` | Block 1 — Section 2.1 | Two-panel 30-bin histogram of listing base price (`price`, $N = 3,818$, Observed Range: $\$20.00\text{--}\$1,000.00$): left panel shows the full distribution (`bins = 30`) with lines for Median ($\$100$), Mean ($\$128.0$), and Upper IQR Bound ($\$262.5$); right panel shows the primary range detail ($\le \$300.00$, $N = 3,659$, $95.8\%$, `bins = 30`) with lines for Median ($\$100$) and Mode ($\$150$). |
| `01_price_by_room_type_boxplot.png` | Block 1 — Section 3.1 | Single-panel vertical boxplot of nightly price across `room_type` (`Entire home/apt`: Median $\$126$, $N = 2,541$, $66.6\%$; `Private room`: Median $\$69$, $N = 1,160$, $30.4\%$; `Shared room`: Median $\$40$, $N = 117$, $3.1\%$), with annotated median tags and observations above category IQR bounds plotted as distinct points. |
| `02_price_by_neighbourhood_group_boxplot.png` | Block 2 — Section 4.1 | Comparative horizontal boxplot of nightly price across all 17 `neighbourhood_group_cleansed` categories ($N = 3,818$), ordered by group median price ($\$70.00$ in `Lake City` to $\$139.00$ in `Downtown`). |
| `02_walkscore_vs_median_price_scatter.png` | Block 2 — Section 4.3 | Neighborhood-level scatter plot of Walk Score vs. median nightly price across the $N = 73$ exact-matched cleansed neighborhoods ($2,733$ listings; Pearson $r = 0.1962$). |
| `03_monthly_price_and_unavailability.png` | Block 3 — Section 5.1 | Two-panel monthly calendar trajectory across 13 month rows (`2016-01` to `2017-01`, $1,393,570$ listing-days): mean and median advertised prices on available listing-days ($n = 934,542$) and unavailable listing-day share across all rows, with partial boundary months (`2016-01`: 28 dates; `2017-01`: 2 dates) explicitly annotated. |
| `03_dayofweek_price_and_unavailability.png` | Block 3 — Section 5.2 | Day-of-week bar chart of mean and median advertised prices on available listing-days (`Monday`–`Sunday`, y-axis starting at $\$0$), highlighting higher Friday–Saturday advertised prices (Mean $\$143.12$) vs. Sunday–Thursday (Mean $\$135.89$). |
| `04_reviews_and_ratings_distributions.png` | Block 4 — Section 6.0 | Three-panel overview of guest reviews and ratings: (A) 30-bin histogram of `number_of_reviews` ($N = 3,818$, Observed Range: $0\text{--}474$, Median $9$, Mean $22.22$, Upper IQR Bound $62$); (B) 30-bin histogram of `review_scores_rating` ($N = 3,171$ valid, Observed Range: $20.00\text{--}100.00$, Median $96.00$, Mean $94.54$); (C) horizontal bar chart of mean and median scores across the 6 review sub-scores (axis starting at $0$, Observed Maximum: $10.00$). |
