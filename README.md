## Methodological Limitations & Modeling Guidelines

- **Respect data grain:** `listings_clean.csv` (listing), `calendar_clean.csv` (listing-day), and `reviews_clean.csv` (review) have distinct grains. Aggregate appropriately before merging to prevent row multiplication.
- **Evaluate data leakage after task definition:** `listings_clean.csv` captures static attributes as of `2016-01-04` and historical review aggregates through `2016-01-03`, whereas `calendar_clean.csv` is a forward-looking snapshot (`2016-01-04` to `2017-01-02`). Data leakage is a risk that must be checked once the prediction target, observation cutoff, and train/test split are defined; any imputation or scaling must be fit on the training split only.
- **Calendar unavailability is an occupancy proxy:** `available == False` ($32.94\%$ of listing-days) cannot distinguish guest bookings from host-blocked dates, and calendar prices are recorded only when `available == True`.
- **Boundary months and event table scope:** `January 2016` (28 dates) and `January 2017` (2 dates) are partial calendar months, and the 18 matched event dates do not represent an exhaustive census of 2016 Seattle events.
- **Walk Score coverage & aggregation level:** Exact name matching leaves $1,085$ listings ($28.42\%$) across 14 neighborhoods unmapped (`<NA>`), boundary equivalence between sources is not verified, and the neighborhood-level correlation ($r = 0.1962$ across 73 neighborhoods) does not describe listing-level relationships.
- **Observational scope:** All EDA comparisons and correlations describe empirical associations in the snapshot and do not establish causal relationships.
